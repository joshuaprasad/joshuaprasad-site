Why the measure matters

Current meta-analytic evidence ranks the structured interview as the most valid selection method available, roughly double the unstructured interview. The same evidence carries a caution: validity varies widely with how well the interview is built. A structured interview is not a list of questions. It is defined constructs, calibrated questions, and anchored scoring. Built well, it's the cheapest reliable assessment in the toolkit. Built casually, it's a conversation.

Sackett, Zhang, Berry & Lievens (2022), Journal of Applied Psychology
