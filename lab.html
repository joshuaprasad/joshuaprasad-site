The Prasad Lab

Researching fairness, authenticity, and the future of work.

The lab studies workplace equity, AI and machine learning in personnel decisions, and authentic employee experiences, with particular attention to fairness in assessment, neurodiversity at work, and broadening participation in STEM careers.

Full publication list: Google Scholar.
